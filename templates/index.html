{{ icon('alert') }} Limitations you should know

  • A prediction reflects word patterns in the training data. It is not proof that a claim is true or false.
  • True statements can look "suspicious" to a model and false ones can look ordinary. Context, sources and evidence matter more than any score.
  • Sentiment models struggle with sarcasm, negation and context. Clusters are unlabeled groupings, not verified categories.
  • Features marked "Demo mode" use a tiny synthetic sample so the app can start. Train on the real dataset for meaningful results.
